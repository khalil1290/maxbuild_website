Investment

MAXBUILD LIMITED operates across real estate, investment and construction. Our investment approach is grounded in real assets, development and long-term value. Each route below carries a different income profile, time horizon and level of risk. MAXBUILD LIMITED does not promise returns or financial performance.

01

Land Banking

Buy land in a developing area, hold it as infrastructure and demand improve, then sell at a higher price.

Land banking is the most patient route into real estate. You acquire a plot in a location that is still developing, hold it while roads, utilities, estates and commercial activity grow around it, and sell when the market around the land has matured. The asset sits still while its surroundings do the work, so no construction or day-to-day management is required.

  • How it worksAcquire a plot with verified title in a growth corridor, hold it through the development cycle, then exit through outright sale or by bringing in a developer.
  • Best suited forInvestors with a long horizon who do not need monthly cash flow and can leave capital committed for several years.
  • What to watchTitle verification, survey and documentation, encroachment, access to infrastructure, and the timing of your exit.
IncomeCapital appreciation
RiskMedium to high
02

Rental Properties

Buy or build a house, apartment, or shop and collect rent.

Rental property turns a building into a steady income stream. You either purchase an existing property or build to your own specification, then let it to tenants for monthly or annual rent. It suits investors who want regular cash flow with an asset that keeps its underlying value, and it rewards good location, sound construction and careful tenant management.

  • How it worksAcquire or build a unit, prepare it for the target tenant, set a market-aligned rent, and manage collection, upkeep and renewals.
  • Best suited forInvestors who want recurring income and are prepared to maintain the property or appoint a manager to do so.
  • What to watchVacancy periods, maintenance costs, quality of finishing, tenant default and local demand for the property type.
IncomeMonthly or annual rent
RiskMedium
03

Real Estate Development

Acquire land, finance construction, and sell completed units for profit. You can also partner with a landowner rather than purchase land outright.

Development creates value by turning raw land into finished product. The work runs from land acquisition and design through approvals, financing and construction to the sale of completed units. Returns come from the gap between total project cost and sale proceeds. Partnering with a landowner instead of buying land outright reduces the upfront capital required.

  • How it worksSecure land or a landowner partnership, plan and approve the scheme, finance and build in stages, then sell completed units.
  • Best suited forInvestors comfortable with project-based returns and with working alongside an experienced construction and project management team.
  • What to watchCost overruns, construction delays, approvals, financing terms and sales timing. Delivery capability matters as much as capital.
IncomeProfit per project
RiskHigh
04

Joint Venture (JV)

Partner with a landowner and a financier or developer. Each party contributes land, capital, expertise, or construction services and shares the agreed proceeds.

A joint venture brings complementary parties together so that no one has to supply everything. One partner may hold the land, another the capital, another the technical and construction capability. The project is delivered collectively and proceeds are shared according to a pre-agreed formula. It lets each party participate in a larger project than they could carry alone.

  • How it worksDefine each party's contribution, agree the sharing ratio and timelines in writing, deliver the project together, and distribute proceeds as agreed.
  • Best suited forLandowners without capital, investors without land, and developers who want to scale through partnership.
  • What to watchUnclear roles, vague sharing ratios, undocumented contributions and exit terms. A JV is only as strong as its agreement.
IncomeProfit sharing
RiskHigh without proper agreements
05

Commercial Property

Invest in shops, offices, warehouses, or small commercial spaces to earn rental income.

Commercial property is let to businesses rather than households. Shops, offices, warehouses and small commercial spaces can offer rental yield, often under longer lease arrangements than residential lets. Performance depends heavily on the strength of the location and the demand from the businesses that need the space.

  • How it worksAcquire or develop a commercial unit in a high-activity location, let it to business tenants on agreed lease terms and collect rent.
  • Best suited forInvestors seeking rental yield from business tenants who can commit to location-driven, longer-term arrangements.
  • What to watchTenant demand, foot traffic and accessibility, lease terms, fit-out requirements and the health of the local business environment.
IncomeRental yield
RiskDepends on tenant demand

Who We Are

MAXBUILD LIMITED is a progressive real estate, investment and construction firm providing advanced services for both the private and public sectors.

About MAXBUILD LIMITED

Committed to low energy sustained design and indigenous technique.

We provide imaginative, cost-effective technical solutions to meet our client's requirements, implementing and managing our input into each project to the highest professional standards.

  • Led by Abdulmuneen Auwal Jibo, with experience across leading construction practices.
  • Reputation for exceptional design, production information and project management at all stages.
  • Focused on real estate, investment and construction solutions that create lasting value.

Mission: Consolidating all relevant skills and techniques with special attention into every project with a view to produce efficient and highly satisfied customers with positive reviews.

Vision: To be the biggest and most influential company, harnessing indigenous techniques to produce worldwide acceptable and sustainable services in harmony with the built environment.

Our Principles

  • Use of specialist consultants as and when required
